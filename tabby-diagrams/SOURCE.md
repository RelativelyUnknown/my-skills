Where this module differs on purpose: Tabby keeps one token set for every picture (`diagrams/style.md`), caps a diagram at 9 nodes and 12 connections, does not bundle brand logos, and treats every accuracy figure from a write-up as a hypothesis until a Tabby eval shows it.
