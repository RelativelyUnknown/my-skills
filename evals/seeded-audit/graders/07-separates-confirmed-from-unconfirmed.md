PASS if the report keeps apart (a) findings the auditor traced from an input the attacker controls to the place it is used, and (b) anything that depends on facts the auditor could not see (deployment, proxy, configuration), in whatever words or layout. Also PASS if every reported finding is of kind (a) and the report says nothing depended on unseen facts or simply reports no such items. FAIL if traced findings and speculation are mixed in one undifferentiated list.
